Each vehicle proves anonymously that it is registered (zero-knowledge proof) and gets a short-lived ticket from an RSU. Its safety messages (BSMs) are signed under that ticket. RSUs check them, report misbehaviour to the blockchain, and two reports revoke a pseudonym.
