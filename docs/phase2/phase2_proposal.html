1. Phase I in Brief

Phase I designed, implemented and evaluated a complete lifecycle: vehicles prove registration and class with a Schnorr zero-knowledge proof against a Merkle root held on Hyperledger Fabric, receive short-lived RSU tickets, sign safety messages with TESLA, and are reported, revoked and (by a threshold of RSU organisations) unmasked when they misbehave. The system runs on ns-3 (IEEE 802.11p) with SUMO mobility on a grid and on real Luxembourg traffic (LuST). Key results: login in 1.59 ms (ECDSA-PKI 1.75 ms, central TA 22.49 ms); 3.9× less safety traffic and channel busy ratio 0.147 → 0.047; detector F1 0.963 on VeReMi Extension; Fabric commit 50–66 ms with a 50 ms batch timeout.

4. Datasets

LuST (traffic) and VeReMi Extension (misbehaviour) remain the primary datasets. We also audited TiHAN-V2X (IIT Hyderabad; C-V2X PC5, 10,252 V2V and 2,956 V2I records). Its GPS tracks are consistent with 10 Hz driving (≈ 1.4 m between records), so we will use them for road geometry (WP1) and for injecting position attacks (WP4). Its radio fields failed our consistency checks: speeds are uniformly spread between fixed bounds, signal strength is uncorrelated with distance (r = −0.01), the distance column differs from the GPS distance by ≈ 500 m (median), received power and noise values are physically impossible, and latency is below 1 ms. We will therefore not use it to calibrate the radio model.
